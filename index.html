About Eben & Co. Limited

Eben & Co. Limited is a Ghanaian diversified business group established to create sustainable value across multiple high-growth sectors.

Through our subsidiaries — Vision Tech Ghana, Eben Agro, Skyline Construction Limited and Sonic Goods — we deliver quality products and services while contributing to national development.
