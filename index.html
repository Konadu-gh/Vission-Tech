Our 4 Companies

1. Adepa Natural Products

Adepa Natural Products is a leading Ghanaian organic skincare brand under Eben & Co. Limited, dedicated to providing 100% natural and chemical-free products made from authentic Ghanaian shea butter, black soap, moringa oil, and essential oils. We specialize in whitening soaps, turmeric soaps, charcoal soaps, shea butter pomades, beard oils, and natural lotions. Our products are carefully handcrafted to nourish, repair, and glow the skin while supporting local women shea nut collectors in Northern Ghana. We are committed to promoting Made in Ghana excellence globally with affordable, effective skincare solutions for all skin types.

Accra, Ghana - Production: Tamale & Accra

2. Vission Tech Printing & Media

Vission Tech Printing and Media is the creative and technological arm of Eben & Co. Limited, offering premium printing services, graphic design, branding, and website development. We print high-quality T-shirts, banners, flyers, posters, business cards, custom mugs, and corporate souvenirs with fast turnaround times. Beyond printing, we build professional business websites, e-commerce stores like this one, and manage social media advertising for businesses across Ghana. Our mission is to empower small and medium enterprises with affordable digital and print solutions that make them stand out. Located in Accra, we serve clients nationwide with reliable delivery and excellent customer service.

Accra - Circle, Ghana | Nationwide Delivery

3. Eben Logistics Services

Eben Logistics Services is a fast, reliable, and affordable delivery and courier service operating under Eben & Co. Limited. We specialize in same-day delivery within Accra, next-day delivery across Ghana, and logistics support for e-commerce businesses. Whether it is delivering Adepa skincare products, printed T-shirts from Vission Tech, or agricultural products, we ensure your goods arrive safely and on time. We offer door-to-door delivery, cash on delivery support, parcel tracking, and bulk delivery for businesses. Our riders are trained, professional, and equipped with motorbikes and vehicles to handle all package sizes. We are your trusted logistics partner for growth and customer satisfaction.

Accra, Kumasi, Tema - Ghana Nationwide

4. Eben Agro & Export

Eben Agro and Export is the agricultural division of Eben & Co. Limited focused on sourcing, processing, and exporting high-quality Ghanaian agricultural products, especially raw unrefined shea butter, shea nuts, moringa seeds, and black soap ingredients. We work directly with women cooperatives in the Northern Region to ensure sustainable sourcing and fair trade practices while maintaining international export quality standards. Our shea butter is 100% pure, Grade A, and highly demanded in Europe, USA, and Asia for cosmetics and food industries. We provide bulk packaging, private labeling, and export documentation support for international buyers seeking authentic Ghanaian agro products with traceability and quality assurance.

Sourcing: Tamale | Export Office: Accra, Ghana
