Our Companies

Adepa Natural Products

Adepa Natural Products creates 100% organic skincare using authentic Ghanaian shea butter, black soap and essential oils. We support local women farmers and deliver chemical-free beauty solutions to customers across Ghana and worldwide with premium quality standards.

Vission Tech Printing

Vission Tech provides professional printing, graphic design and website development. From T-shirts and banners to full e-commerce websites, we help businesses brand themselves with fast turnaround, affordable pricing and excellent customer service throughout Ghana.

Eben Logistics

Eben Logistics offers reliable same-day and nationwide delivery services. We handle parcels, bulk goods and e-commerce orders with tracking, cash on delivery and professional riders to ensure your products reach customers safely and on time.

Eben Agro Export

Eben Agro specializes in sourcing and exporting premium Ghanaian shea butter and agricultural products. We work with cooperatives to ensure fair trade, quality control and international export documentation for global buyers seeking authentic African products.
