Welcome to Eben & Co. Limited

Eben & Co. Limited is a Ghanaian holding company focused on natural products, technology, logistics and agro. We create jobs, empower women, and deliver quality "Made in Ghana" products to the world.

About Eben & Co. Limited

We are a diversified Ghanaian company committed to producing natural, FDA-approved products and providing tech solutions that impact lives. Founded in Accra, we operate 4 subsidiaries serving thousands of customers.

Our Vision

To be Africa's leading brand for natural wellness products and technology solutions, made in Ghana for the world.

Our Mission

To produce high-quality, affordable natural products and tech services that empower communities, create jobs, and promote healthy living.
