ABOUT US

More Than a Business.

Eben & Co. Limited is a Ghanaian sustainability company based in Accra. We started with one mission: turn Ghana's underused bamboo into wealth for farmers, jobs for youth, and clean products for the world.

Today we operate bamboo plantations, produce eco-products, and sell across Ghana (Jumia) and internationally (Amazon). We are building Africa's first bamboo industrial park.
