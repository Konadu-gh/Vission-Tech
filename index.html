Our 4 Companies

1. Adepa Natural Products

Adepa Natural Products is a leading Ghanaian organic skincare brand dedicated to providing 100% natural and chemical-free products made from authentic Ghanaian shea butter, black soap, moringa oil, and essential oils. We specialize in whitening soaps, turmeric soaps, charcoal soaps, and body lotions. Our products are handcrafted to nourish and glow the skin while supporting local women shea nut collectors in Northern Ghana.

2. Vission Tech Printing

Vission Tech Printing and Media offers premium printing services, graphic design, branding, and website development. We print T-shirts, banners, flyers, business cards, and custom mugs. Beyond printing, we build professional business websites, e-commerce stores, and manage social media advertising for businesses across Ghana. Our mission is to empower SMEs with affordable digital and print solutions.

3. Eben Logistics Services

Eben Logistics Services provides fast, reliable, and affordable delivery and courier services. We specialize in same-day delivery within Accra, next-day delivery across Ghana, and logistics support for e-commerce businesses. We offer door-to-door delivery, cash on delivery support, parcel tracking, and bulk delivery for businesses. Our riders are trained professionals equipped to handle all package sizes.

4. Eben Agro & Export

Eben Agro and Export focuses on sourcing, processing, and exporting high-quality Ghanaian agricultural products, especially raw unrefined shea butter and shea nuts. We work directly with women cooperatives to ensure sustainable sourcing and fair trade practices. Our shea butter is 100% pure, Grade A, and demanded in Europe, USA, and Asia. We provide bulk packaging and export documentation support.
